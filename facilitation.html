I'm an Atlanta-based strategy consultant who facilitates executive workshops and offsites. 18+ years across private, public, and nonprofit sectors. Hundreds of sessions.

At Deloitte, I designed and facilitated executive sessions inside the Greenhouse—Deloitte's purpose-built immersive environment, grounded in group dynamics, environmental psychology, and innovation theory. I've been doing the same work independently since, for organizations of every size, from the U.S. State Department to community nonprofits.

Imagine a boring "strategic offsite," and now imagine how great it would be if it had gone as well as possible. That's what I do.

My style is warm, structured, and honest. I keep the room moving and name the thing everyone's thinking but nobody wants to say.

Background: MBA from Emory (Woodruff Scholar), mechanical engineering from Brown. Atlanta native. At various times I've taught classes on workshop facilitation, volunteer management, swing dancing, and non-dualistic mindfulness. I enjoy meditation, cocktails, and travel—sometimes all at once.

Every session is custom-designed. I don't bring a binder and run the same playbook—I learn your organization, talk to the people who matter, and build a session around what will actually change things.

What stays constant is the rigor underneath:
