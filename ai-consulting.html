What I help people do

Every engagement is different, but the work usually falls into a few categories.

Get your daily work done faster

Email, writing, research, meeting prep, follow-ups—the stuff that eats your day. I help you set up AI workflows that handle the repetitive parts so you can focus on the parts that need your brain.

Write better, in your own voice

AI can draft, edit, and rework your writing—but only if it knows your voice. I help you build a style guide and feedback loop so your AI writes like you, not like a robot.

Integrate AI into your business

Client intake, proposals, reporting, scheduling—small businesses run on processes that AI can streamline. I help you figure out which ones are worth automating and build the systems to do it.

Build tools with AI ("vibe-coding")

You don't need to be a developer to build useful software anymore. I help non-technical people design and create custom tools—dashboards, trackers, internal apps—using AI-assisted development.

A real engagement, start to finish

What a single setup session actually looks like.

From ChatGPT User to AI-Powered Workflow

Nonprofit executive · Washington, DC · Single 2-hour session

Before: A senior nonprofit leader who'd been using ChatGPT for about a year. Typed prompts, got answers, moved on. No persistent memory, no integrations, no reusable workflows.

What we built together:

  • Connected AI to her Gmail. It read 500+ sent emails and produced a writing style guide that captured her actual voice and patterns.
  • Set up safety guardrails so AI can draft but never send on her behalf.
  • Ran a multi-perspective review of a blog draft using three AI “expert sub-agents,” each critiquing from a different professional lens.
  • Scoped a competitive intelligence system: automated monitoring of who’s shaping the conversation on her organization’s key issues.

After: A system that knows her voice, connects to her tools, and picks up where she left off. Two hours to go from “ChatGPT is useful” to “this is a working partner.”

Every engagement starts differently. This one started with email and writing. Yours might start with meeting prep, client intake, or building a custom tool.
