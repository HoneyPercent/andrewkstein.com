A real engagement, start to finish

What a single setup session looks like.

From Stage 1 to Stage 5 in two hours

Nonprofit executive · Washington, DC · Single 2-hour session

Before: Using ChatGPT for about a year. Typed prompts, got answers, moved on. No persistent memory, no integrations, no reusable workflows. Pure Stage 1.

What we built:

  • A starter CLAUDE.md — her role, her voice, her clients, her current priorities — so the AI picks up where she left off each session.
  • Connected the AI to her Gmail; it read 500+ sent emails and produced a writing style guide that captured her actual voice.
  • Set up safety guardrails so the AI can draft but never send on her behalf.
  • Ran a multi-perspective review of a blog draft using three AI “expert sub-agents,” each critiquing from a different professional lens.
  • Scoped a competitive-intelligence workflow: automated monitoring of who's shaping the conversation on her organization's key issues.

After: A system that knows her voice, connects to her tools, and picks up where she left off. Stage 5. Two hours.

That was the foundation. The full Starter Package adds the breadth — more skills, more integrations, a deeper CLAUDE.md, the workflows that fit her actual week.

Every engagement starts differently. This one started with email and writing. Yours might start with meeting prep, client intake, or building a custom tool.
