Why a top-level Games tab

  • Discoverability. A daily habit only works if people can find it without hunting. Top nav = front door.
  • The funnel. Free game → “read the story behind the answer” → a real israeled.org article → CIE+. The game is the widest mouth of the funnel; it belongs at the top, not nested.
  • Room to grow. One tab holds a whole catalog — Guess The Year today, Connections and Pinpoint later — the way NYT Games grew from Crossword to a dozen titles.
  • Brand fit. “Know your past” becomes something you do daily, not just read.
