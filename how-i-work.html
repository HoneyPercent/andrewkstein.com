AI Consulting

I build AI systems around how you actually work.

Not a course. Not a webinar. I sit down with you, learn your workflows, and build the tools and habits that make AI useful for your specific work. You walk away with a system you actually use.

What I help people do

Every engagement is different, but the work usually falls into a few categories.

Get your daily work done faster

Email, writing, research, meeting prep, follow-ups—the stuff that eats your day. I help you set up AI workflows that handle the repetitive parts so you can focus on the parts that need your brain.

Write better, in your own voice

AI can draft, edit, and rework your writing—but only if it knows your voice. I help you build a style guide and feedback loop so your AI writes like you, not like a robot.

Integrate AI into your business

Client intake, proposals, reporting, scheduling—small businesses run on processes that AI can streamline. I help you figure out which ones are worth automating and build the systems to do it.

Build tools with AI ("vibe-coding")

You don't need to be a developer to build useful software anymore. I help non-technical people design and create custom tools—dashboards, trackers, internal apps—using AI-assisted development.

Not sure where you fit?

Most people start with "I know AI can help, I just don't know where." That's exactly what the intro call is for. We'll talk about your work and I'll tell you honestly whether I can help.

Who I typically work with

  • Writers and creators who want AI to handle research, drafts, and editing without losing their voice
  • Small business owners drowning in admin work that could be systematized
  • Consultants and professionals who bill for their time and want more of it back
  • Nonprofit leaders doing the work of three people and looking for leverage
  • Curious people who've tried ChatGPT a few times and know there's more to it

My background is mechanical engineering (Brown) and an MBA (Emory, Woodruff Scholar), plus 6 years at Deloitte and hundreds of clients across five continents. I'm not a developer—I'm a consultant who builds things. That means I think about your problems the way you do, not the way an engineer does.

How the engagement works

  1. Free intro call — 30 minutes

    We talk about what you do, what's frustrating, and whether AI can actually help. No sales pitch. If I'm not the right fit, I'll tell you.

  2. Deep dive — 2 hours

    I map your workflows, understand your tools, and identify the biggest opportunities. You leave this session with a clear plan for what we'll build.

  3. Build sessions — 3 × 90 min

    We build and test your system together. Custom templates, AI workflows, automation—whatever makes the biggest difference for your work. You're hands-on the whole time, so you know how everything works when we're done.

  4. Ongoing support — optional

    AI evolves fast. A monthly retainer keeps your setup current and lets you add new workflows as your needs change.
