The 1967 War

Six days that redrew the Middle East. Speeches, briefings, and U.N. exchanges leading into and out of the war.

In the weeks before June 5, 1967, the closure of the Straits of Tiran, the expulsion of UNEF, and the massing of Egyptian forces in Sinai forced Israel to decide whether to wait or strike. The articles below walk that decision in primary sources — Eshkol's address to the nation, Rabin's briefings, Rafael at the U.N., and Resolution 242 in its aftermath.

Quick read on tradeoffs:

  • A — Hero bar: highest signal that audio exists; lowest detail. Best if "press play and let it run" is the dominant use case.
  • B — Inline card: shows the curated arc. Best if Ken wants the playlist to feel like an edited product, not an automated feature.
  • C — Right rail: the only layout that supports read-and-listen-together. Most engineering load; mobile collapse needs design.
  • D — Banner + per-article: least disruptive. Pairs the topic-level "play all" CTA with article-level entry points. Closest to the current page.

Probably worth combining: B's track list inside the topic page + D's per-article play buttons on each article card + a single sticky playbar at the bottom (Burke's existing cie-audio-bar__*).
